Pingza Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Pingza is a WhatsApp Business messaging and campaign management platform under development. This policy explains how we handle information when providing our services.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, we may process account details such as names and email addresses; business and WhatsApp account identifiers; phone numbers and contact lists; message content, templates and delivery statuses; campaign activity; technical logs and security information; and subscription or billing records.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and maintain the service, connect authorized WhatsApp Business Accounts, manage contacts and campaigns, send requested messages, process opt-outs, provide support, secure accounts, calculate usage fees and meet legal obligations.

3. WhatsApp and Meta

When you connect a WhatsApp Business Account, Pingza may process information made available through Meta's APIs according to the permissions you grant. Meta independently processes information under its own policies and terms.

4. Sharing

We may share necessary information with service providers supporting hosting, storage, messaging, security and billing, or disclose it where legally required. We do not sell personal information for money.

5. Retention and Security

We intend to use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards. Information should be retained only as long as necessary for the stated purposes, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of eligible personal information by contacting us. We will verify requests as appropriate and handle them under applicable law. Certain information may need to be retained for legal, security or dispute-resolution purposes. Disconnecting WhatsApp does not necessarily delete data already stored by Pingza or Meta.

7. Customer Responsibilities

Customers must have the necessary rights and permissions to upload contact information and send messages, and must respect applicable consent, opt-out and WhatsApp messaging requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Pingza is intended for business use and is not designed for children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the service develops. Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
